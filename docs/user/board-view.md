# Board view

Board view gives you a compact overview of active sessions across your connected environments.
Open it with **Board view** in the sidebar or visit `/board`.

Sessions are grouped by project. Each card shows the project context, environment, branch, runtime,
model, and current status. Select a card to open that session's regular thread; Board view does not
create a separate copy of the thread or its state.

Settled, snoozed, and archived threads stay out of Board view. Sessions that need your attention,
such as a pending approval or input request, remain visible.
